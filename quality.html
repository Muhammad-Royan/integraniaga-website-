Moisture Control & Supply Capability

Prevention of Caking & Scalable Supply

Organic coconut sugar is naturally hygroscopic. Without strict moisture control, international ocean freight can lead to moisture absorption, caking, and cargo spoilage.

As an authorized B2B export trading enterprise, we mitigate this risk entirely. We exclusively curate our products from certified local suppliers capable of strictly maintaining a maximum moisture content of 2.0%, ensuring the granules remain free-flowing upon arrival at your destination port.

Our consolidated supplier network across Central Java ensures a scalable supply capacity of up to 100 Metric Tons (MT) / Month, allowing us to consistently fulfill large-scale international wholesale contracts.
