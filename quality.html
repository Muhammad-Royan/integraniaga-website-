Quality Control & Compliance

Mitigating procurement risks through rigorous quality assurance and strict adherence to international food safety standards.

Our Quality Assurance Role

As your dedicated sourcing partner, PT Integra Niaga Komoditas acts as your first line of defense in quality control. We do not compromise on specifications. We ensure consistent Indonesian coconut sugar quality through meticulous producer curation, rigorous pre-shipment inspections, and strict export preparations for international markets.

Our Quality Standards

Laboratory Specifications

We strictly control our sourcing to meet maximum 2.0% moisture content and standard Mesh 16-18 particle size to ensure physical stability during maritime transit.

Authenticity & Purity

100% natural processing with no additives. The purity of our curated coconut sugar is verifiable via C13 Isotope Testing to guarantee zero cane sugar adulteration.

Microbiological Safety

Every batch we curate is required to pass strict food safety thresholds, ensuring negative results for harmful pathogens such as Salmonella and E. Coli.

Our Sourcing & QC Process

1. Producer Curation

Selecting and auditing trusted local farmer cooperatives that align with our hygienic processing standards.

2. Pre-Shipment Inspection

Conducting thorough quality checks (moisture, color, and mesh sizing) before the packaging phase begins.

3. Secure Export Loading

Supervising container stuffing and palletization to ensure the cargo remains intact and moisture-free during international shipment.

Global Compliance & Certifications

As a strategic sourcing partner, we match your procurement needs with the right facilities. Certifications such as USDA Organic, EU Organic, Fair Trade, Halal, and HACCP can be provided depending on the selected manufacturing facility and your specific market compliance requirements.
