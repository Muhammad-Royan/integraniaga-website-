Moisture Control

Prevention of Caking & Moisture Absorption

Organic coconut sugar is naturally hygroscopic. Without strict moisture control, international ocean freight can lead to moisture absorption, caking, and cargo spoilage.

As a professional B2B export trading enterprise, we mitigate this risk entirely. We exclusively curate our products from certified local suppliers capable of strictly maintaining a maximum moisture content of 2.0%, ensuring the granules remain free-flowing upon arrival at your destination port.
