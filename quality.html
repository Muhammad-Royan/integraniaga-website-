Moisture Control & Supply Capability

Prevention of Caking & Scalable Supply

Organic coconut sugar is naturally hygroscopic. Without strict moisture control, international ocean freight can lead to moisture absorption, caking, and cargo spoilage.

As an authorized B2B export trading enterprise, we mitigate this risk entirely. We exclusively curate our products from certified local suppliers capable of strictly maintaining a maximum moisture content of 2.0%, ensuring the granules remain free-flowing upon arrival at your destination port.

Our consolidated supplier network across Central Java ensures a scalable supply capacity of up to 100 Metric Tons (MT) / Month, allowing us to consistently fulfill large-scale international wholesale contracts.

Farm-to-Port Control

5-Stage Production & Quality Workflow

Every step of processing across our curated supplier network is monitored against strict international food safety benchmarks.

STEP 01

Fresh Nectar Tapping

Morning sap collection from certified organic coconut groves in Central Java with natural pH monitoring to prevent premature fermentation.

STEP 02

Hygienic Boiling

Controlled thermal evaporation in food-grade stainless vessels without synthetic preservatives, bleaching agents, or sulfites.

STEP 03

Drying & Granulation

Precision drying and mechanical sifting through 16–18 mesh stainless screens to achieve uniform, free-flowing crystals.

STEP 04

Moisture & Metal Check

Digital moisture analyzer verification (≤ 2.0%) and metal-detector screening prior to final bulk packaging.

STEP 05

Hermetic Sealing

Immediate heat-sealing inside food-grade PE inner liners and multi-wall kraft bags or export cartons for container stuffing.

Food Safety & Lab Testing

Every export lot undergoes strict laboratory verification to meet North American, European, and Asian import regulations:

  • 100% Purity Guarantee: Zero cane sugar blending, zero maltodextrin, zero sulfites, and no synthetic anti-caking agents.
  • Microbiological & Heavy Metal Compliance: Tested against international food-grade thresholds (TPC, Yeast & Mold, Salmonella, Lead, Arsenic).
  • Batch Certificate of Analysis (CoA): Issued for every shipment with exact moisture, sucrose, and mesh readings.

Full Batch Traceability & Retention

Transparency is central to our role as a commercial bridge between Central Java farming cooperatives and global food manufacturers:

  • Unique Lot Coding: Every 20kg–25kg bag carries a traceable batch number linking back to the processing facility and production date.
  • Pre-Shipment Retention Samples: Sealed reference samples are retained for quality verification and buyer audit confidence.
  • Third-Party Inspection Ready: Full cooperation with independent surveyors (such as SGS, Sucofindo, or Intertek) upon buyer request.
