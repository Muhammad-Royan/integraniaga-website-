About PT Integra Niaga Komoditas

Your Trusted Commercial Bridge for Premium Organic Commodities

Headquartered in Pemalang, Central Java, PT Integra Niaga Komoditas operates as a dedicated export trading enterprise specializing in unrefined, organic granulated coconut sugar. We manage an unbroken, fully traceable supply chain sourced directly from traditional farmer cooperatives.

By maintaining rigorous laboratory standards, moisture limits strictly capped at max 2.0%, and professional international container logistics, we guarantee uncompromised quality for global food manufacturers, importers, and distributors.
