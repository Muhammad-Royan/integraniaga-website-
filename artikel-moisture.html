Why Moisture Max 2.0% is Crucial for Coconut Sugar Export

Published by PT Integra Niaga Komoditas • Quality Assurance Division

When engaging in international B2B trade of organic coconut sugar (gula semut), one of the most critical technical parameters that determines product viability is moisture content. At PT Integra Niaga Komoditas, we strictly enforce a maximum moisture threshold of 2.0% for every export batch dispatched to global markets.

1. The Hygroscopic Nature of Coconut Sugar

Organic coconut sugar is naturally hygroscopic, meaning it has a high affinity for absorbing moisture from the surrounding air. During long-distance maritime transit across different climate zones—moving from the tropical humidity of Indonesian ports to cooler or temperate destination ports in Europe, North America, or East Asia—uncontrolled moisture can cause severe physical and chemical changes.

2. Preventing Caking and Microbiological Degradation

If moisture levels exceed the safe 2.0% threshold, the sugar granules begin to dissolve at their microscopic edges and recrystallize. This leads to hardening, caking, and large block formations inside the packaging, making the product entirely unserviceable for industrial food processors, bakeries, and beverage manufacturers. Furthermore, elevated humidity creates an ideal environment for mold and bacterial proliferation, resulting in complete shipment rejection by destination customs.

3. Our Rigorous Quality Control Mitigation

To guarantee that our organic coconut sugar arrives in pristine condition, PT Integra Niaga Komoditas implements strict protocols:

  • Controlled Evaporation: Working closely with certified farmer cooperatives to ensure proper thermal processing during sap reduction.
  • Advanced Mechanical Drying & Sifting: Sorting the granules to uniform 16-18 mesh sizes while stabilizing moisture content below 2.0%.
  • Export-Grade Multi-Layer Packaging: Utilizing multi-wall kraft paper bags equipped with double-sealed food-grade PE inner liners and container desiccant packs to block humidity during sea freight.

By uncompromisingly maintaining this technical standard, PT Integra Niaga Komoditas ensures long shelf-life stability and total reliability for our international wholesale partners worldwide.
