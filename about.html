Our Enterprise Identity

Established Exporter of Organic Coconut Sugar from Pemalang

Based in Pemalang, Central Java, Indonesia, PT Integra Niaga Komoditas was established as a dedicated export trading enterprise focusing on high-grade organic granulated coconut sugar. We serve international buyers, food manufacturers, wholesale distributors, and retail brands seeking authentic, sustainable, and laboratory-tested organic sweeteners.

Our Vision

To be the leading and most trusted global supplier of premium organic coconut sugar from Indonesia. We envision a sustainable future where our high-quality natural sweeteners empower global food industries while elevating the livelihood of traditional farmers in Central Java.

Our Mission

  • Quality Assurance: Delivering 100% pure organic coconut sugar that meets strict international standards (HACCP, GMP, FDA).
  • Direct Partnership: Working directly with verified local farmer cooperatives in Pemalang to ensure fair trade and ethical sourcing.
  • Global Reliability: Providing seamless B2B export experiences, transparent tracking, and reliable logistics for wholesale buyers worldwide.
