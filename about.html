Our Roots & Purpose

Curating Certified Excellence for the Global Market

PT Integra Niaga Komoditas was born from a profound recognition of the rich agricultural heritage across Central Java. Seeing the massive global demand for premium coconut sugar, we identified the critical need for a highly professional commercial bridge to connect this exceptional regional potential with strict international market standards.

Operating as an authorized B2B export trading enterprise, our strength lies in meticulous curation. We partner exclusively with fully certified, export-ready local suppliers across the region. This strategic sourcing model allows us to guarantee product consistency, strict moisture control (≤ 2.0%), and absolute compliance with international food safety certifications.

Our ultimate value to global wholesale buyers is risk mitigation and supply chain reliability. By navigating the complex local landscape and consolidating only the highest-grade certified organic sugar, we provide our international partners with a seamless, scalable, and worry-free sourcing experience directly from Indonesia.

Our Vision

To be the leading and most trusted global supplier of premium organic coconut sugar from Indonesia. We envision a sustainable future where our meticulously curated natural sweeteners empower global food industries while elevating the livelihood of traditional farming communities across Central Java.

Our Mission

  • Quality Assurance: Delivering 100% pure organic coconut sugar sourced strictly from facilities that meet international standards (HACCP, GMP, FDA).
  • Strategic Sourcing: Working with verified and certified local suppliers across the region to ensure scalable volume and consistent product integrity.
  • Global Reliability: Providing seamless B2B export experiences, transparent tracking, and reliable logistics for wholesale buyers worldwide.
