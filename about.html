About PT Integra Niaga Komoditas

Specialized agricultural trading and export enterprise bridging skilled Indonesian farmer cooperatives directly with international B2B markets.

Our Business Model

Specialized Trading & Global Sourcing Partner

PT Integra Niaga Komoditas operates as a premier agricultural trading and export enterprise. We partner directly with verified local producer cooperatives and skilled farmers across Indonesia to source natural organic coconut sugar granules (gula semut).

As an expert trade partner, we supply international markets consistently, catering to direct importers, national and regional distributors, organic ingredient suppliers, food manufacturers, beverage corporations, commercial bakeries, and confectionery industries worldwide.

Accredited Producer Quality Assurance

Our export shipments originate from verified producer facilities backed by internationally recognized organic certifications including USDA and EU Organic standards.

We enforce strict technical specifications (maximum moisture content 2.0%, mesh 16-18) to guarantee premium quality during global container shipping.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted and sustainable exporter of Indonesian organic agricultural commodities, empowering local farmer communities through global market connectivity.

Our Mission

Delivering uncompromised export quality organic coconut sugar, maintaining strict food safety standards, and fostering long-term transparent partnerships with international B2B buyers.
