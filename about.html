About PT Integra Niaga Komoditas

Connecting ethical Indonesian agricultural sources with global manufacturing standards.

Empowering Local Farmers, Supplying Global Markets

PT Integra Niaga Komoditas is an officially registered export trading enterprise based in Indonesia. We specialize in sourcing, processing, and exporting natural coconut sugar and agricultural commodities directly to food and beverage corporations worldwide.

Our direct partnership model with rural farming cooperatives ensures fair remuneration for harvesters while guaranteeing absolute transparency, traceability, and batch consistency for international importers.
