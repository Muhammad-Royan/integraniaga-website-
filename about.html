About Our Company

Bridging the gap between skilled Indonesian coconut sugar producers and the demands of the global market through transparent supply chain management.

Specialized Sourcing & Supply Chain Partner

PT Integra Niaga Komoditas is a specialized trading company serving as the vital bridge between local Indonesian coconut farmers and global B2B markets. We focus exclusively on the curation, quality control, and export of premium natural coconut sugar.

We pride ourselves on our transparent supply chain management. We partner directly with selected local farmer cooperatives, conducting strict quality control to ensure every batch meets international food safety and technical standards before it leaves the port.

Our expertise lies in mitigating the risks of international procurement. From handling export documentation (such as Certificate of Origin and Bill of Lading) to managing maritime logistics and export-ready packaging, we provide a frictionless sourcing experience for global food manufacturers and distributors.

Our Commitment & Values

Strict Quality Control

Implementing rigorous standardization procedures (such as ensuring Max 2.0% moisture and standard mesh sizes) before any cargo is loaded for shipment.

Logistics Excellence

Providing seamless supply chain solutions and flexible shipping terms (FOB, CFR, EXW) tailored to international buyers' needs.

Transparent Partnership

Building ethical, long-term, and mutually beneficial relationships with both our local farmer networks and our international clients.
