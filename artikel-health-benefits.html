Nutritional Superiority and Low Glycemic Index (GI 35)

Published by PT Integra Niaga Komoditas • Quality Assurance Team

In the modern food and beverage manufacturing sector, health-conscious consumer segments are constantly evaluating the nutritional profiles of sweetening ingredients. Organic coconut sugar has gained immense international popularity due to its distinct nutritional advantages over conventional white cane sugar and artificial sweeteners.

1. Low Glycemic Index Advantage (GI 35)

Unlike regular table cane sugar, which typically possesses a high glycemic index ranging from 60 to 65, pure organic coconut sugar boasts a significantly lower glycemic index of approximately 35. This lower GI value means that glucose is absorbed into the bloodstream at a much slower, more gradual rate, preventing sudden insulin spikes and making it an exceptional ingredient for health-focused product formulations.

2. Retention of Essential Trace Minerals

Because coconut sap undergoes minimal chemical processing compared to highly refined cane sugar, it retains valuable trace nutrients naturally present in the coconut palm nectar. These include potassium, magnesium, zinc, iron, and dietary fibers like inulin, which aids digestion.
