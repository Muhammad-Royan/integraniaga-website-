Export Ready Packaging & Logistics

Reliable packaging and container loading solutions designed to protect coconut sugar quality and prevent moisture during long-distance maritime shipments.

Our Standard Bulk Packaging

25 kg Multi-layer Kraft Paper Bag

The industry standard for bulk export. Equipped with a food-grade PE Inner Liner to provide an excellent moisture barrier, preventing the sugar from clumping during transit.

20 kg / 25 kg Premium Carton Box

Equipped with a Food-Grade Plastic Liner. This option is highly recommended for buyers requiring superior stacking stability and maximum protection in strict warehouse environments.

Logistics & Export Preparation

As your dedicated sourcing partner, we ensure that every shipment is meticulously prepared, palletized, and loaded to maximize efficiency and safety from the port of loading to your destination.

Container Capacity (FCL)

Optimized loading capacity: Approx. 17 - 18 Metric Tons for a 20ft Container, and up to 25 - 26 Metric Tons for a 40ft Container (without pallets).

Palletization Standard

We provide ISPM-15 certified wooden pallets or plastic pallets upon request, securely shrink-wrapped to meet your specific warehouse unloading requirements.

Private Label & Customization

Need retail packaging or your own brand printed on the bags? We facilitate custom packaging dimensions and private label (OEM) solutions for our long-term partners.

Contact us to discuss your preferred incoterms (FOB, CFR, CIF), shipment quantity, and specific packaging requirements.
