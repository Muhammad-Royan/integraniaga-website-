Packaging Integrity

Multi-Layer Protection Against Moisture & Contamination

Because organic coconut sugar is naturally hygroscopic, improper packaging during international ocean freight can lead to moisture absorption, caking, and cargo spoilage. At PT Integra Niaga Komoditas, we enforce a robust multi-layered packaging standard.

Every batch is packed securely to withstand varying humidity levels across international shipping lanes, ensuring that the product arrives at destination ports in pristine, free-flowing condition.

Standard Export Packaging Specifications

Designed for optimal container utilization and wholesale storage.

Packaging Layer Material & Specification
Inner Barrier Food-grade, heat-sealed Polyethylene (PE) inner liner bag
Outer Container Multi-wall Kraft Paper Bags (Strong tear-resistant structure)
Standard Net Weight 25 kg per bag (Custom retail pouches available upon request)
Container Stuffing (FCL) Optimized for 20ft / 40ft Full Container Load with industrial desiccants
