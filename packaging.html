Standard Export Packaging Options

We provide premium, food-grade packaging solutions designed for optimal container utilization, safe handling, and maximum physical protection during international shipping.

Option 1

Multi-Layer Kraft Paper Bag

  • Net Weight: 25 kg per bag
  • Outer Layer: Multi-wall Kraft Paper (Providing strong, tear-resistant structure for heavy-duty transit).
  • Inner Layer: Food-grade Polyethylene (PE) Inner Liner, securely heat-sealed.
Option 2

Premium Carton Box

  • Net Weight: 20 kg or 25 kg per box (Customizable)
  • Outer Layer: Premium, thick corrugated Carton Box for maximum stacking stability.
  • Inner Layer: Food-grade Plastic Liner, securely tied or sealed.

Anti-Humidity Container Prep

Every container undergoes pre-loading inspection for dryness and odor neutrality, lined with protective kraft paper and equipped with industrial container desiccants to prevent condensation ("container rain").

Export Shipping Marks & Traceability

Each bag or carton is clearly printed with batch lot numbers, production & expiry dates, net/gross weight, organic certification status, and custom importer shipping marks for effortless warehouse intake.

Custom Bulk Bag Printing & Wrapping

We accommodate custom importer branding, private-label printing on 20kg–25kg bulk kraft bags or master cartons, and heavy-duty shrink-wrapping for maximum pallet stability during FCL transit.

Logistics & Palletization
